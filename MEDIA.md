All items are freely licensed via Wikimedia Commons; CC BY-SA items require attribution,
which is also rendered in the UI footer of each page that shows media.
Media depicts real personnel at public events/ceremonies/exercises — used purely as
visual placeholder texture in a SIMULATED surveillance demo. No person shown is a
suspect or watchlist target; demo names/tracks are fictional.
